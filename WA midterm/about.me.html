About me

I am a student-athlete majoring in economics at the University of Colorado, Boulder. I thrive in fast-paced environments and have a strong interest in how systems—whether in sport, design, or society—can be optimized and improved. My background in competitive skiing has shaped how I approach challenges: with discipline, curiosity, and a long-term perspective.

Alongside athletics, I am drawn to creative and analytical work. I enjoy exploring the intersection of data, design, and human behavior, and I am particularly interested in projects that combine structure with creativity.

This website is a space to bring these parts of my life together. It documents my academic work, creative projects, and personal interests, while also serving as a portfolio and a place for reflection.

Background

Born and raised in northern Sweden, outdoors and skiing was a part of my lifestyle from childhood. That environment shaped my relationship with competition early on, eventually leading to opportunities to compete at the big stage. I have represented Sweden in multiple Junior World Championships and World Cup event. This experience taught me discipline, consistency, and "grit". My path then brought me to the U.S. to compete as a D1 athlete while pursuing my degree in Economics. I started my collegient jurney at Montana State University. This transformation pushed me to adapt to new terrain, a new culture, and a new balance between athletics and academics. During my time at Montana State, I was recognized with MVP honors, and a U.S. National champion title. This experience reinforced the value of leadership, reliability, and showing up for the people around you, not just yourself. Today, I compete at the Division I level for University of Colorado after transfering in 2024. while continuing my academic work, carrying with me a background shaped by international competition, collegiate athletics, and constant transitions. Adaptability is a key word to me. Adapting to all the circomstances that life throws at you. Across countries, teams, and disciplines, I have learned how to stay grounded, curious, and committed — both in sport and beyond it.

Interests

My interests span a variety of areas. I am a pasionated trail runner, and loves a long day in the mountains. In terms of academics, I decided to pursue a minor in creative technology and design as I have always been drawn to the intersection of creativity and structure. With this minor, I get to explore how design, technology, and human behavior interact, and it gives room for creative expression alongside my analytical studies in economics. I enjoy working on projects that combine data, design, and user experience, as they together shape how we interpret a message. Beyond academics and athletics, I am a social person who values connections and community. I enjoy spending time with friends, exploring new places, and engaging in activities that bring people together.

Goals

2026 involves a transitional period for me as I will graduate and move on from collegiate athletics. My immediate goal is to successfully complete my degree in economics while making the most of my final year as a student-athlete. Beyond graduation, I aim to leverage my background in economics and my experiences as an athlete to pursue a career that combines analytical thinking with creativity.
