About me

I am a student-athlete studying economics, with a strong interest in how systems—whether in sport, design, or society—can be optimized and improved. My background in competitive skiing has shaped how I approach challenges: with discipline, curiosity, and a long-term perspective.

Alongside athletics, I am drawn to creative and analytical work. I enjoy exploring the intersection of data, design, and human behavior, and I am particularly interested in projects that combine structure with creativity.

This website is a space to bring these parts of my life together. It documents my academic work, creative projects, and personal interests, while also serving as a portfolio and a place for reflection.

Background

Born and raised in northern Sweden, outdoors and skiing were part of my everyday life. That environment shaped my relationship with competition early on, eventually leading to opportunities to compete on a bigger stage. I have represented Sweden at multiple junior world championships and world cup events. This experience taught me discipline, consistency, and grit.

My path then brought me to the U.S. to compete as a Division I athlete while pursuing my degree in economics. I started my collegiate journey at Montana State University. This shift pushed me to adapt to new terrain, a new culture, and a new balance between athletics and academics.

During my time at Montana State, I was recognized with MVP honors, an experience that reinforced the value of leadership, reliability, and showing up for the people around you—not just yourself.

Adaptability is a key word for me: adapting to the circumstances that life throws at you. Across countries, teams, and disciplines, I have learned how to stay grounded, curious, and committed—both in sport and beyond it.

Interests

My interests span a variety of areas. I am a passionate trail runner and love a long day in the mountains. I decided to pursue a minor in creative technology and design because I have always been drawn to the intersection of creativity and structure.

Through this minor, I explore how design, technology, and human behavior interact, and it gives room for creative expression alongside my analytical studies in economics. I enjoy working on projects that combine data, design, and user experience—because together they shape how we interpret a message.

Beyond academics and athletics, I am a social person who values connection and community. I enjoy spending time with friends, exploring new places, and doing activities that bring people together.

Goals

2026 involves a transition for me as I graduate and move on from collegiate athletics. My immediate goal is to successfully complete my degree in economics while making the most of my final year as a student-athlete.

Beyond graduation, I aim to leverage my background in economics and my experiences as an athlete to pursue a career that combines analytical thinking with creativity.
